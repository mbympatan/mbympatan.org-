About MBYM Patan

Building an inclusive and empowered Deaf community.

Our Mission

MBYM Patan works for the welfare, rights, accessibility, education, sports, social participation and empowerment of Deaf people in Patan and surrounding areas.

Our Focus

Indian Sign Language (ISL), accessible information, community programs, sports, cultural festivals, social help and opportunities for Deaf success.

Our Programs

Community activities for participation, inclusion and opportunity.

🏆 Sports

Deaf sports activities, tournaments, coaching and participation.

💃 Navratri

Accessible Garba and cultural celebrations for the Deaf community.

🎨 Holi Festival

Inclusive community celebrations and social participation.

🤝 Seva Camp

Service camps and volunteer support for the community.

❤️ Social Help

Support, awareness and assistance for Deaf people and families.

🌟 Deaf Success

Promoting education, employment, skills and achievements.
