About

MBYM Patan works for the empowerment, welfare and inclusion of the Deaf community in Patan and surrounding areas.

Our work promotes:

Indian Sign Language (ISL) accessibility
Education and awareness
Deaf sports and cultural activities
Social welfare and community support
Employment and skill development
Accessible heritage and public information
Deaf community participation and success

Accessibility

MBYM Patan supports accessible communication and the use of Indian Sign Language (ISL) so that Deaf people can access information and participate equally in society.
